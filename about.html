◆ The Beginning

Who We Are

Shawarma Reem was born from a simple belief: that great shawarma requires nothing more than the finest ingredients, an honest recipe, and real care in every wrap.


We started as a small shawarma spot in Amman, serving our neighbourhood with one goal — to make the kind of shawarma that brings people back, not because they have to, but because they genuinely want to.


Today, Shawarma Reem is a trusted name in Amman. Our menu is built on two things: chicken and meat shawarma, each perfected through years of practice and a dedication to quality that never wavers.


Whether you walk in for a quick regular sandwich or sit down for a triple mix meal with the family, you will always leave satisfied — that is our promise.
